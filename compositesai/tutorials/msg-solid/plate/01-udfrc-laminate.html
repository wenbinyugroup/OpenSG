01 UDFRC cross-ply laminate#

Step 1: The fiber-resolved plate cell, its ABD and G#

Prompt

Build an OpenSG plate SG of a [90/0/90/0] laminate (listed from the bottom) with the fibers resolved: a 1 × 1 × 4 mm 3-D cell of four 1 mm layers, one fiber per layer through its thickness, square pack, Vf = 40%, 0° fibers along x1 and 90° fibers along x2, periodic in x1 and x2. Fiber E = 276 GPa, ν = 0.28, matrix E = 4.76 GPa, ν = 0.37, in N, mm, MPa. Homogenize it with OpenSG’s Reissner-Mindlin plate model and give the ABD matrix and the transverse shear stiffness matrix G. Show the mesh.

CompositesAI

Built and homogenized the periodic resolved-fiber \(1 \times 1 \times 4\) mm [90/0/90/0] plate SG. The mesh has 32,000 hex elements and exact meshed \(V_f=40\%\).

Rows/columns of the Reissner–Mindlin \(ABD\) matrix are \([e_{11},e_{22},\gamma_{12},\kappa_{11},\kappa_{22},\kappa_{12}]\):

\[\begin{split}\begin{bmatrix} 2.56881e5&1.79156e4&0.00251&6.15160e5&3.58366e4&0.00495\\ 1.79156e4&2.56881e5&0.00251&3.58258e4&4.12365e5&0.00508\\ 0.00251&0.00251&1.66999e4&0.00487&0.00516&3.33999e4\\ 6.15160e5&3.58258e4&0.00487&1.76294e6&9.46289e4&0.01140\\ 3.58366e4&4.12365e5&0.00516&9.46289e4&9.51765e5&0.01224\\ 0.00495&0.00508&3.33999e4&0.01140&0.01224&9.08483e4 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Units by block: \(A\) in N/mm, \(B\) in N, and \(D\) in N·mm. The nonzero \(B\) block is expected for this unsymmetric stacking sequence.

\[\begin{split}G = \begin{bmatrix} 9612.08 & 0.00230\\ 0.00230 & 9819.66 \end{bmatrix} \ {\rm N/mm}\end{split}\]

The stiffness is symmetric and positive definite. The mesh, checked SG file, and full stiffness/compliance results are attached.
