02 Corrugated steel sheet with face sheets#

Step 1: The sheet's ABD: CompositesAI asks for the flat lengths#

Prompt

Use OpenSG to find the plate stiffness (ABD) of a trapezoidal corrugated steel sheet: period 101.6 mm, mid-line height 25.4 mm crest to trough, web angle 45 deg, thickness 2 mm, E = 210 GPa, nu = 0.3, in N, mm and MPa. Show the SG mesh image.

CompositesAI

Please specify the mid-line crest and trough flat lengths. Their sum must be \(50.8\ \text{mm}\). Should I assume a symmetric profile, with both lengths \(25.4\ \text{mm}\)?

Step 2: Equal flats: the ABD and the mesh#

Prompt

yes

CompositesAI

Completed classical OpenSG plate homogenization. The mesh image is corrugated_steel_mesh.png; full results are in corrugated_steel_ABD.md.

Assumptions used: symmetric profile with 25.4 mm crest and trough flats; \(x_1\) along corrugation channels and \(x_2\) across the 101.6 mm period. Reference plane is the mid-height plane.

\[\begin{split}\begin{bmatrix}N\\M\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}A&B\\B&D\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix}\epsilon\\\kappa\end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Rows/columns: \((\epsilon_{11},\epsilon_{22},\gamma_{12},\kappa_{11},\kappa_{22},\kappa_{12})\).

\[\begin{split}\mathbf{ABD}= \begin{bmatrix} 5.07102e5&3.89995e2&0&0&0&0\\ 3.89995e2&1.29998e3&0&0&0&0\\ 0&0&1.34623e5&0&0&0\\ 0&0&0&5.00763e7&3.83585e4&0\\ 0&0&0&3.83585e4&1.27862e5&0\\ 0&0&0&0&0&6.51861e4 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Block units: \(A\) in N/mm, \(B\) in N, \(D\) in N·mm. The omitted entries are numerical zero (below \(7\times10^{-8}\) in their respective units).

  • \(A_{11}=507{,}102\) N/mm, \(A_{22}=1{,}300\) N/mm, \(A_{66}=134{,}623\) N/mm

  • \(D_{11}=5.0076\times10^7\) N·mm, \(D_{22}=1.2786\times10^5\) N·mm, \(D_{66}=6.5186\times10^4\) N·mm

  • \(B\approx0\), as expected about the mid-height reference plane.

The 496-element quadratic mesh is periodic and the stiffness matrix is symmetric positive definite.

Step 3: Face sheets added: the ABD and the mesh#

Prompt

Add [45/-45]s facesheet of UD/epoxy lamina prop at both top and bottom. Have each ply of thickness 0.125mm. assume UD/epoxy prop and compute ABD matrix, and publish png image

CompositesAI

Added identical 0.5 mm \([45/-45]_s\) UD/epoxy facesheets to both sides. Mesh image: corrugated_steel_ud_faces_mesh.png. Results: corrugated_steel_ud_faces_ABD.md.

Assumed UD carbon/epoxy lamina: \(E_1=181{,}000\), \(E_2=E_3=10{,}300\), \(G_{12}=G_{13}=7{,}170\), \(G_{23}=3{,}680\) MPa; \(\nu_{12}=\nu_{13}=0.28\), \(\nu_{23}=0.40\).

Classical ABD, rows/columns \((\epsilon_{11},\epsilon_{22},\gamma_{12},\kappa_{11},\kappa_{22},\kappa_{12})\):

\[\begin{split}\begin{bmatrix} 5.65451e5&4.83461e4&1.87883e1&0&0&0\\ 4.83461e4&7.67521e4&6.26276e1&0&0&0\\ 1.87883e1&6.26276e1&1.81710e5&0&0&0\\ 0&0&0&6.13104e7&8.96488e6&8.95783e3\\ 0&0&0&8.96488e6&1.34564e7&2.82966e4\\ 0&0&0&8.95783e3&2.82966e4&1.11697e7 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

\(A\): N/mm; \(B\): N; \(D\): N·mm. The \(B\) block is numerical zero (largest magnitude \(1.25\times10^{-6}\) N), consistent with symmetric facing about the mid-height plane. The 1,312-element periodic mesh passed symmetry and positive-definiteness checks.
