03 UDFRC cross-ply laminate#

Step 1: The UD ply and the laminate section#

Prompt

Build an OpenSG 2-D solid beam cross-section of a [90/0/90/0] laminate (listed from the bottom): 18 mm wide, four 1 mm layers, centred at the origin, beam axis x1. Each layer is a UD ply of fiber E = 276 GPa, ν = 0.28 and matrix E = 4.76 GPa, ν = 0.37 at Vf = 40%, square pack. Homogenize the ply from its fiber and matrix first, then lay the 0° plies with their fibers along x1 and the 90° plies with their fibers along x2. Units N, mm, MPa. Show the meshes.

CompositesAI

Built and homogenized the centred 18 × 4 mm \([90/0/90/0]\) Timoshenko solid-beam section. Both requested meshes are shown above.

UD ply homogenization (square packing, requested \(V_f=40\%\); meshed \(V_f=39.968\%\)):

  • \(E_1=113.180\) GPa, \(E_2=E_3=13.195\) GPa

  • \(G_{12}=G_{13}=3.960\) GPa, \(G_{23}=3.117\) GPa

  • \(\nu_{12}=\nu_{13}=0.32896\), \(\nu_{23}=0.42535\)

Beam stiffness (N, mm, MPa):

  • \(EA=4.57969\times10^6\) N

  • \(GA_2=2.35748\times10^5\) N

  • \(GA_3=1.76085\times10^5\) N

  • \(GJ=1.30156\times10^6\) N·mm²

  • \(EI_2=2.47881\times10^6\) N·mm²

  • \(EI_3=1.23418\times10^8\) N·mm²

The section is symmetric about the origin; tension and shear centres are effectively at \((y_2,y_3)=(0,0)\) mm. The full stiffness, compliance, and mass matrices are supplied in Beam stiffness and mass matrices. Mass is zero because density was not provided.

Assumptions used: \(\nu_{23}=0.40\) for the constituent transverse-isotropic completion was not needed because the supplied fibre and matrix were treated as isotropic; constituent/ply density was set to zero.

Step 2: Euler-Bernoulli and Timoshenko beam laws#

Prompt

Homogenize this section with OpenSG’s solid beam model. Give the Euler-Bernoulli 4×4 and the Timoshenko 6×6 stiffness and compliance, and the tension and shear centres.

CompositesAI

Homogenization complete for the solid beam section (288 quad9 elements).

Euler–Bernoulli — order \([e_{11},\kappa_1,\kappa_2,\kappa_3]\)

\[\begin{split}\mathbf K_\mathrm{EB}= \begin{bmatrix} 4.57969e6&0&8.63910e{-10}&2.74266e{-8}\\ 0&1.30156e6&4.80029e{-11}&0\\ 1.22131e{-9}&4.80029e{-11}&2.47881e6&4.69097e{-9}\\ 2.71456e{-8}&0&4.59063e{-9}&1.23418e8 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]
\[\begin{split}\mathbf S_\mathrm{EB}= \begin{bmatrix} 2.18355e{-7}&0&0&0\\ 0&7.68311e{-7}&0&0\\ 0&0&4.03419e{-7}&0\\ 0&0&0&8.10255e{-9} \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Timoshenko — order \([e_{11},\gamma_{12},\gamma_{13},\kappa_1,\kappa_2,\kappa_3]\)

\[\begin{split}\mathbf K_\mathrm{T}= \begin{bmatrix} 4.57969e6&0&0&0&0&0\\ 0&2.35748e5&0&0&0&0\\ 0&0&1.76085e5&0&0&0\\ 0&0&0&1.30156e6&0&0\\ 0&0&0&0&2.47881e6&0\\ 0&0&0&0&0&1.23418e8 \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]
\[\begin{split}\mathbf S_\mathrm{T}= \begin{bmatrix} 2.18355e{-7}&0&0&0&0&0\\ 0&4.24182e{-6}&0&0&0&0\\ 0&0&5.67908e{-6}&0&0&0\\ 0&0&0&7.68311e{-7}&0&0\\ 0&0&0&0&4.03419e{-7}&0\\ 0&0&0&0&0&8.10255e{-9} \end{bmatrix}\end{split}\]

Stiffness uses the consistent N–mm–MPa generalized units for these strain/curvature orders. Reported off-diagonal terms are below \(3\times10^{-8}\) in magnitude and shown as zero above.

  • Tension centre: \((y_2,y_3)=(-5.99\times10^{-15},\,1.89\times10^{-16})\) mm

  • Shear centre: \((y_2,y_3)=(-4.70\times10^{-17},\,3.63\times10^{-14})\) mm

Both centres therefore coincide with the section origin to numerical precision.

Files supplied: Euler-Bernoulli beam law (laminate_EB_law.md), Timoshenko beam law (laminate_Timo_law.md), and their checked OpenSG models (laminate_EB.yaml, laminate_Timo.yaml).
