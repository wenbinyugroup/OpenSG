Step 1: Local stresses under torsion and bending#

Editor note

This note is from the editors of this page. It is not part of CompositesAI's reply.

The table below is labelled MPa. Its rows \(S_{22}\), \(S_{33}\) and \(S_{23}\) are in Pa, in both the Min and the Max column. The bar's model is in SI units, with lengths in m and moduli in Pa. OpenSG wrote its stresses in Pa. These three rows were not converted to MPa. In MPa they read:

  • \(S_{22}\): −2.129×10⁻¹² to +1.899×10⁻¹² MPa

  • \(S_{33}\): −7.646×10⁻¹³ to +7.099×10⁻¹³ MPa

  • \(S_{23}\): −3.766×10⁻¹³ to +4.061×10⁻¹³ MPa

The rows \(S_{11}\), \(S_{13}\) and \(S_{12}\) are in MPa, as labelled. So are the contour plots. The downloadable stress recovery summary gives the same values under the same MPa label. Its rows \(S_{22}\), \(S_{33}\) and \(S_{23}\) are in Pa too.

The transverse normal/shear components \(S_{22}\), \(S_{33}\), and \(S_{23}\) are numerical zero for this isotropic, traction-free solid section. Torsion produces \(S_{12}\) and \(S_{13}\).

Bending-stress check

\[I_2=\frac{0.040(0.020)^3}{12}=2.6667\times10^{-8}\ \text{m}^4, \qquad c=0.010\ \text{m}\]
\[\sigma_\text{max}=\frac{M_2c}{I_2} =\frac{100(0.010)}{2.6667\times10^{-8}} =37.50\ \text{MPa}.\]

OpenSG’s reported element-mean peak is 35.625 MPa at \(y_3=\pm9.5\) mm. This matches the analytical stress at the element-centroid sampling position; the physical extreme-fibre value is 37.50 MPa.

The recovered moments are \(M_1=50.039\) N·m and \(M_2=100.071\) N·m, within 0.078% and 0.071% of the applied values. The detailed summary is in stress_recovery_summary.md.
