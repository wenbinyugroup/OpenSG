Tutorials#

The tutorials come from four conversations with CompositesAI. The prompts are what the user typed, and the responses are CompositesAI's replies as they came. Tutorials 01 and 02 follow an aluminium bar as a beam, and 02 continues 01. Tutorials 03 and 04 follow a corrugated steel sheet as a plate, and 04 continues 03. Tutorials 05 and 06 follow the shell section of a NACA 4418 airfoil as a beam, and 06 continues 05. Tutorials 07 and 08 follow the shell section of the IEA-22 blade at r = 0.2 as a beam, and 08 continues 07. Tutorial 02 carries one editor note, set apart in a box, on a unit label in CompositesAI's reply.
