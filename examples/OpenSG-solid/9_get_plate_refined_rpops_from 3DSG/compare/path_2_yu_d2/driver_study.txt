reading
-------
1. The driver inputs are now EXACT to the macro plate solution: the
   spectral d2eps satisfy the moment-equilibrium identity to 4.8e-7
   and reproduce the plate's true transverse-shear-gradient split
   (gamma1,1, gamma2,2) to 0.00% -- the 5%-low stencil and the 40%
   gamma1,1 misallocation of the LS drivers are both eliminated.
2. Yet the crescent rms barely moves: S11 14.9 -> 14.7, S22 41.3 ->
   41.0, S12 6.5 -> 6.6, S13 135.1 -> 134.3, S33 17.7 -> 18.7, S23
   99.7 -> 105.9.  Driver error is therefore NOT the accuracy
   bottleneck of the pure-Yu composition on this path: the hypothesis
   that the S13 channel rode on the gamma1,1 misallocation is
   DISPROVEN (exact split bought 0.8 points of 134).
3. The constrained-LS row shows the same thing from the other side:
   forcing the drivers to exact equilibrium while distorting their
   pointwise values made every component worse -- the recovery
   responds to the local d2eps values, not to the integral identity.
4. What remains is junction cell-scale content the macro derivatives
   cannot carry: the crescent path crosses the facesheet/core junction
   where the true 3-D field has cell-boundary layers outside the
   periodic-SG function space driven by ANY macro polynomial content.
   S22/S23/S13 sit on that content; S13 is additionally
   reference-limited (C3D4 scatter floor 88% of signal).  The C3D10
   reference job currently running will arbitrate how much of the
   remaining gap is reference discretization vs genuine model content.
